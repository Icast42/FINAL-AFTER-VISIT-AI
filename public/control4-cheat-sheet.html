Control4 in 4 taps

Picture on the screens, sound on the surround system. That's all you need. Follow the steps in order.

Log in→ 2nd icon on left→ All One Main Screen→ Audio Sources→ Close
1

Wake the tablet & log in

Turn the tablet on, unlock it, and open the Control4 app. Log in if it asks.

Hold the tablet sideways (landscape). The menu icons run down the left edge.

2

Tap the 2nd icon on the left

That's the room/home button. It opens TV 1 with four big tiles.

If something looks wrong

Screens are black
All One Main Screen → Power Toggle Wall. Give the TVs a few seconds.
Roku is stuck in an app
All One Main Screen → Home Screen - All Roku, then pick your Roku again.
Picture works, no sound
Audio Sources → pick the Press Box that matches what's on the screen (see table above).
Wrong sound playing
Audio Sources → pick the right one. The new choice replaces the old one.
Lost in the menus
Tap Close, then tap the 2nd icon on the left to get back to TV 1.
Watch and Video Wall aren't needed for everyday use. Still stuck? Ask a manager.
